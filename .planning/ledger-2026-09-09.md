## What shipped server-side (PR #6)

- `bot_channel_members` table; `ChannelService::is_member` is principal-agnostic; `add_bot_member`,
  `remove_bot_member`, `bot_channel_ids`, `list_for_bot`.
- `POST/GET /channels/{cid}/bots`, `DELETE /channels/{cid}/bots/{bid}` — caller must be a channel
  member who is channel admin or workspace owner/admin; DMs refused.
- Bot tokens accepted (`BotOrUser`) on channel/message/thread/reaction routes; bot sends stamped
  `sender_type: "bot"`; E2EE plaintext rejection applies to bots; emails blanked for bots.
- Bot WebSocket principals pre-subscribe from `bot_channel_members`; `ws_tickets`/`reactions` FKs to
  `users` dropped (bot ids allowed) with explicit cleanup in `BotService::delete`.
- Slash commands: `slash_commands` table, admin CRUD (`signing_secret` once, `url` hidden from
  non-admins), `POST /channels/{cid}/commands` → `command.invoked` over the owning bot's socket when
  connected, else signed HTTP dispatch (URL validated against loopback/private/link-local, no
  redirects, 64 KiB cap, 4096-byte reply truncation).
- Deleting a bot evicts its auth-cache entry **and** disconnects its live socket.
- `fixtures/wire/server-events.json` generated from serde; a non-exhaustive `match` fails the build
  when a `ServerEvent` variant gains no sample.
